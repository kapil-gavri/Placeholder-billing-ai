The product

Practical tools for outpatient Clinics.

The appointment book, reception desk and consultation queue work from the same information.

01

Schedules and appointments

Set Doctor consulting windows, breaks, closures and capacity. Authorized staff can book, reschedule, cancel and add walk-ins.

02

Check-in and live queue

Reception checks patients in while Clinic staff operate the queue using the Clinic’s configured schedule and Token order.

03

Clinic-owned WhatsApp

When enabled by a Clinic, patients can receive requested booking confirmations, reminders and queue updates from that Clinic’s number.

04

Role-based access

Clinic administrators, receptionists and Doctors receive controls appropriate to their responsibilities without access to unrelated Clinics.

How it works

From appointment request to completed visit.

1

The Clinic configures its schedule

Doctor availability, consulting windows, breaks and closures remain under Clinic control.

2

An appointment is requested

Clinic staff can book directly. Where WhatsApp is enabled, a patient can follow the Clinic’s guided booking journey.

3

The patient receives operational updates

Appointer sends only the appointment and queue communications the patient agreed to receive.

4

The Clinic operates the visit

Reception and Doctors manage check-in, calling, consultation and completion from the Clinic dashboard.

Privacy and trust

Limited to appointment and queue coordination.

Appointer is operated by Livi Enterprises. It is not a medical-record, diagnostic, payment or emergency service.

Clear WhatsApp consent

Patients are told which Clinic will message them and what updates to expect. Replying STOP ends future automated updates; START resumes them.

No unsolicited outreach

Marketing, spam, purchased contact lists, misleading identities and unrelated outreach are prohibited.

Data minimisation

The controlled product does not collect clinical notes, diagnoses, prescriptions, insurance information or patient payment details.

Accessible controls

Read our Privacy Notice, Terms, or data-deletion instructions.
