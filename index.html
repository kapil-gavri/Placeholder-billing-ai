PRODUCT

The practical tools a Clinic needs each day.

Appointer keeps the appointment book, reception desk and consultation queue working from the same information.
01

Schedules and appointments

Set each Doctor’s consulting windows, breaks, closures and capacity. Staff can book, reschedule, cancel and add walk-ins.

02

Check-in and live queue

Reception checks patients in while authorized staff and Doctors operate the queue in scheduled Token order.

03

Private patient updates

Patients receive confirmations, private appointment links and useful turn updates without creating an Appointer account.

04

Role-based Clinic access

Clinic admins, receptionists and Doctors get the controls they need without exposing unrelated Clinic data.

ABOUT

Built to reduce uncertainty in outpatient care.

Appointer is a worldwide product for coordinating appointments and live patient queues at one physical Clinic location. It is designed to keep staff in control, keep patients informed and limit unnecessary disclosure of patient information.

Appointer does not store clinical notes, diagnoses, prescriptions or medical records. Patient payments and automated subscription billing are not part of the controlled pilot.
