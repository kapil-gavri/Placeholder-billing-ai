Privacy Notice · Updated 6 September 2026

Your information stays limited to running appointments and Clinic queues.

Appointer is operated by Livi Enterprises. Clinics use Appointer to manage their own schedules, appointments, patient communications, and live queues. Questions can be sent to support@appointer.in.

Information we process

For Clinic users, we process account identity, Clinic and Doctor details, verification documents, access roles, and operational activity. For patients, we process the patient name, WhatsApp mobile number, Clinic, Doctor, appointment time, booking status, Token, queue events, consent time, and message delivery status. We also process limited security and reliability logs.

Why we use it

We use this information to create and manage appointments, operate the Clinic queue, provide private status links, send requested confirmations and reminders, protect accounts, prevent abuse, support users, and meet applicable legal obligations. Appointer does not sell patient information or use appointment information for advertising.

Who receives it

The Clinic and its authorized staff can access the information needed to run that Clinic. Infrastructure and communication providers process limited information for Appointer, including Cloudflare for hosting, the configured email provider for login codes, and Meta/WhatsApp when a Clinic enables WhatsApp messaging. Those providers also apply their own terms and privacy notices.

WhatsApp choices

Before confirming a WhatsApp-enabled booking, the patient is told which Clinic will message them and what appointment updates to expect. Reply STOP in that Clinic’s chat to stop future automated WhatsApp updates; reply START to resume. Stopping messages does not cancel an existing appointment.

Retention and security

The default patient-identifying retention period is three months. After that, identifying appointment information is anonymized while limited non-identifying operating statistics may remain. Private patient links are random, expire after the visit lifecycle, and are revoked when appointment information is deleted. Access is role-scoped by Clinic.

Your choices

You may cancel or reschedule through your private link when available, withdraw WhatsApp consent, or ask the Clinic to access, correct, export, or delete appointment information. You may also follow our data deletion instructions.

Product boundary

Appointer is an appointment and queue coordination service, not a medical-record or emergency service. This version does not collect clinical notes, diagnoses, prescriptions, insurance information, or patient payment details.
