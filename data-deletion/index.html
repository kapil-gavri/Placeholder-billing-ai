Data Deletion

Request deletion of Appointer information.

Appointer is operated by Livi Enterprises. Privacy requests can be sent to support@appointer.in.

Patients

Ask the Clinic that created your appointment, quoting the booking ID shown in your confirmation or private status link. You may also email support@appointer.in with the Clinic name and booking ID. Do not email medical information, passwords, verification codes, or identity documents.

Clinic users

A Clinic administrator can delete individual appointment information from Appointer’s privacy controls and export Clinic data. To close a Clinic account or request broader deletion, email support from the registered account address with the Clinic name.

What happens next

We or the Clinic may ask for limited information to verify the request and prevent unauthorized deletion. Eligible identifying information is deleted or anonymized, and private patient links are revoked. Some security, audit, or legal records may be retained where necessary and then removed according to the applicable retention period.

For other privacy questions, read the Privacy Notice.
