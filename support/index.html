Appointer Support

How can we help?

Appointer is operated by Livi Enterprises. For Clinic setup, account access, verification, appointment coordination, WhatsApp messaging, privacy, or data-deletion questions, contact:

support@appointer.in

When contacting us

Include your Clinic name and a short description of the issue. Patients may include the booking ID shown in their confirmation. Do not email passwords, verification codes, medical information, government identity documents, or WhatsApp access tokens.

Privacy and deletion

Read our Privacy Notice or follow the data deletion instructions.

Emergency notice

Appointer provides appointment and queue coordination. It is not a medical or emergency service. For urgent medical help, contact an appropriate local emergency service or healthcare provider.
